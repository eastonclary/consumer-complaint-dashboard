Executive summary

Published complaints nearly doubled, from 2,734,268 in 2024 to 5,442,931 in 2025. Credit reporting accounted for most of the increase. Across the remaining products, complaints about attempts to collect debt not owed led both years. The timely-response share fell slightly.

Operational Insights

Complaint concentration

Credit reporting contributed 2,444,705 of the 2,708,663 additional published complaints. For workload planning, monitoring that product separately makes smaller changes elsewhere easier to see.

Beyond credit reporting

Across other products, attempts to collect debt not owed rose from 70,171 to 115,682 complaints. Its share fell from 19.03% to 18.29% as other issues grew faster in aggregate. Both count and share matter when prioritizing review.

Response timeliness

The timely share across published complaints fell by 0.17 percentage points. Among completed cases, it fell by 0.05 points. The difference illustrates how the case population affects an operational metric.

Methodology and limitations

Population and dates

The source is the CFPB Consumer Complaint Database, downloaded as the November 8, 2026 snapshot. The inclusive receipt-date filter runs from January 1, 2024 through December 31, 2025. One unique Complaint ID contributes one record. Monthly counts use receipt month.

How to read complaint volume

CFPB publishes eligible complaints sent to companies after a response or the publication waiting period. Certain administrative responses and referrals to other regulators are excluded. Counts cannot establish unique consumers, verified violations, prevalence of harm, or company performance. Customer and market-share denominators are unavailable.

Category definitions

Original product and issue labels are retained. CFPB lists form revisions in 2023 and 2026, outside these receipt years. The exclusion view removes the exact product “Credit reporting or other personal consumer reports.” Issues are ranked within the selected population. CFPB classification history

Data quality and geography

All in-scope records were retained, including missing fields. The cleaning log records 509,714 partial or masked ZIPs and 43,910 fully masked ZIPs. These 553,624 records contribute to volume. No five-digit location was inferred. The subset had zero malformed ZIPs and zero reversed receipt/sent dates.

Metric definitions and reporting context

Annual growth uses 2024 as the base. Product contribution divides the product’s count change by the overall net increase. Issue shares use all complaints in the chosen scope and year, including missing issue labels. Combined-volume rankings use the sum of both years. Source counts are integers and percentages are rounded for display.

Response-stage shares use all published cases in the year. Closed-outcome shares use only explicitly completed cases. “In progress,” “Untimely response” and missing outcomes remain separate. Timeliness uses the separate “Timely response?” field: Yes divided by Yes plus No for the selected population. All records have a known flag. A timely response does not establish relief or consumer satisfaction.

One credit-reporting submission can concern multiple agencies. CFPB discusses third-party submissions, online advice, and changes directing consumers to dispute inaccuracies with reporting agencies first. Its annual report does not date that change precisely enough to explain a particular monthly increase. The report covers a broader intake population and a different snapshot. 2025 Consumer Response Annual Report
